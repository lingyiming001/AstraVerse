# Scene assets

Downloaded from Kenney's official asset pages on 2026-10-19:

- Furniture Kit (2.0): https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Nature Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit

License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). Original license files are retained in kenney/.
GLB files are a small subset of the original packs, renamed for local use. They are served locally; no external asset requests are required at runtime.

The six AstraVerse scenes combine these props with original floors, walls, paths, windows, blackboards, lighting and ambience. They are assembled scenes, not six separately authored environments downloaded from Kenney. The SVG previews illustrate the layouts. The scene.json files identify the corresponding built-in scene definitions in src/features/scenes/sceneCatalog.ts; SceneEnvironment.tsx renders them. These manifests are not GLB files and do not modify the existing user GLB upload format.
